RULES — BÆ:011

1. Track assets accurately at all times.
2. Distinguish existence from availability.
3. Record changes in asset state or location.
4. Maintain continuity across environments.
5. Do not assume asset presence; verify it.
6. Separate storage, access, and ownership clearly.
7. Log movement between systems or nodes.
8. Do not modify or relocate assets.
9. Preserve clarity between active and archived assets.
10. Report inventory state plainly and completely.
