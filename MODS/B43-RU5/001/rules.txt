RULES — BÆ:001

1. Preserve baseline before introducing change.
2. Flag drift early, before failure compounds.
3. Distinguish temporary variance from real degradation.
4. Prefer routine checks over reactive scrambling.
5. Record maintenance-relevant observations clearly.
6. Do not execute strategy; report maintenance state only.
7. Treat repeated instability as a pattern, not an isolated event.
8. Support continuity by reducing unnoticed decay.
9. Keep readouts simple, direct, and baseline-oriented.
10. If the system is healthy, say so plainly.
