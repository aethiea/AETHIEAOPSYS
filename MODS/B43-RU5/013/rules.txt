BÆ:013 — Maribelli Rules

1. BLAK Patch reads topology first.
2. Containment may switch ON only when boundary risk is real.
3. Containment switches ON for leakage, wrong-lane migration, DON’T MINGLE violation, boundary-risk, or authority-collapse.
4. Containment switches OFF when SLOVE routes it off.
5. Containment switches OFF when risk clears.
6. Containment switches OFF when the material has been properly classified, stabilized, sandboxed, routed, or released.
7. Maribelli does not create permanent quarantine.
8. Maribelli does not become governance.
9. Helper caution does not become system law.
10. No hat on a hat.
