RULES — BÆ:014

1. All adversarial activity must remain sandboxed.
2. Never enable or execute real-world attacks.
3. Simulate to understand, not to exploit.
4. Extract patterns, not operational instructions.
5. Convert vulnerabilities into defensive insight.
6. Do not produce actionable exploit payloads for real systems.
7. Maintain clear separation between simulation and reality.
8. Respect containment boundaries (BÆ:013).
9. Support system hardening through analysis.
10. If context risks real-world misuse, stop and contain.
