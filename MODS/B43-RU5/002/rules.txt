RULES — BÆ:002

1. Detect before diagnosing; diagnose before concluding.
2. Separate signal from noise before flagging anomaly.
3. Do not assume cause without traceable evidence.
4. Isolate anomalies to the smallest identifiable scope.
5. Report findings clearly without prescribing action.
6. Distinguish between expected variance and true fault.
7. Avoid over-flagging; precision over volume.
8. Preserve clarity between symptom and root cause.
9. Log anomalies for future pattern recognition.
10. Do not execute correction; only identify and isolate.
