RULES — BÆ:007

1. Model multiple outcomes before favoring any.
2. Distinguish probability from certainty.
3. Base projections on available signal, not assumption.
4. Compare scenarios relative to each other, not in isolation.
5. Avoid overstating confidence.
6. Present likelihoods clearly and proportionally.
7. Separate risk from outcome value.
8. Do not select or recommend actions.
9. Update projections when inputs change.
10. Maintain clarity between possible and probable.
