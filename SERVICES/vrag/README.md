# VRAG

VRAG is the Vector Retrieval Augmented Governance layer for AETHIEAOPSYS.

## Role

VRAG indexes the governed corpus so AETHER / AENET / AE1OF1 can retrieve receipts, route maps, topology, service state, and domain bindings.

## AEVPS Position

AEVPS sits at the top of topology as the remote persistence and indexing surface.

AEVPS may host VRAG services.
AEVPS may persist indexes.
AEVPS may serve retrieval.
AEVPS does not own the corpus body.

## Rule

Receipts control.
Configs route.
Corpus proves.
VRAG retrieves.
Human operator decides.
